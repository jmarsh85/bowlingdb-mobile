Vanquish™ Symmetric

Featuring the Vanquish Symmetrical core originally featured in the Revolt line in weights 16#-14#, this powerhouse produces an explosive 2.47 RG to increase rev potential and .056 differential for aggressive track flare.  This combination delivers unstoppable strength and control.  In 13#-12#, the symmetrical Quadfire™ core is featured for fantastic specs, even at the lower weight.

Leverage HFS Reactive

The Turmoil HFS solid cover found 12 years ago on the original Covert Revolt was a favorite.  But the game has evolved.  Oil has gotten heavier and thicker leaving the original cover in need of an upgrade.  The new Leverage HFS Solid cover offers the same length and control as the Turmoil HFS cover, but with added strength for modern oil conditions.   When paired with the Vanquish core, this cover provides enough length through the front of the lane with plenty of traction in the mid-lane for a smooth and controllable ball motion and nonstop drive through the pins.

    Smooth, Very Smooth

    I had a feeling with such a massive weight block wrapped in the modified Black Venom goodness, that we might be working with a Grand Slam Walk Off Home Run Ball. Sure enough, that is exactly what we have here.

    Knowing the release of this ball was going to coincide with my leaving for Reno to go bowl nationals. I kicked the box surface off of it and immediately went to 2k wet sand. While I don't leave for Reno for a few days, I couldn't resist and had to crack the shell of it and put a couple of games on it. 224 was middling with a 7-10 and a couple of other 10's but following with 279 and 237 after some modest moves to maximize shape and get it in the right part of the lane truly showed where it shines. At least for me with this layout and surface it didn't have the big bang hockey stick shape. It was smooth, very mid lane, arc shape. Easy to control and predict and it's really nice to have that shape at my disposal, especially for when I start seeing Wet/Dry Over/Under House Shot Nonsense.

    For the record, My average speed is right around 17.5mph out of hand and 15.25 to 15.5 at the pins. My rev rate is not what it once was but still clocks in between 325-350. My PAP is 4 3/4 Over and 1 3/4 Up.

    I would recommend this to a friend
